Cover pictures for reports and slides
=====================================

Put a picture here (PNG, JPG or GIF) and it appears in the Report generator's
Cover tab (Workbook > Report generator...). It is cropped from the middle to
the shape of the space (a wide strip along the top of the report's first page,
along the bottom of the title slide) and never stretched, so a wide picture
works best, about 4:1 for the PDF.

To use a picture without copying it here, choose "Use my own picture..." in the
Cover tab.
